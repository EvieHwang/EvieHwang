# Evie's Sake Log

_Last updated: 2026-06-27_

A personal record of sake tried, what it taught me about my taste, and what to chase next. Maintained via the `sake` skill.

## Taste profile

- **Why sake:** It agrees with me where wine doesn't (no headache), and an evening glass rounds out the day. Keeps well open, too.
- **Stated entry preference:** light, clean, fresh — which sent me toward polished Junmai Ginjo / Daiginjo first.
- **Discovery (2026-06-27):** A humble, full-bodied, rice-forward junmai (Kizakura "Pure") landed as one of the best I've tried — the opposite end of the spectrum from my daiginjo baselines. Honest, unfussy, no ginjo perfume in the way of the rice itself.
- **Working hypothesis:** My real pull may be toward rich, savory, rice-forward junmai over light/clean aromatic daiginjo. Being tested, not settled.
- **Likely rewards to chase:** kimoto / yamahai junmai (traditional-method depth and savory tang), gentle warming (nurukan), true Yamada Nishiki junmai.

## To try next

- **Kimoto / yamahai junmai** (e.g. Kurosawa Junmai Kimoto) — the traditional-method version of the style I loved tonight.
- **Kizakura "Pure" served warm** (nurukan, ~40°C) — same bottle, to watch the rice character bloom.
- **A true Yamada Nishiki junmai** — the better-rice version of the Pure, now that there's a baseline to judge against.
- **Otokoyama Tokubetsu Junmai** — drier, earthier savory contrast to test the edges of the hypothesis.
