## Active right now

- **SOGI data governance (G2) — this year's priority.** Agreement with Sam to raise
  it at the **August meeting**, with action to be planned afterward. Pre-August, Evie
  is preparing a distribution-limited conversation-starter to share with Sam and a
  select group of four commissioners — **Romero, Co-Chair Lozano, Jeke, Archer**
  (five including Evie = the OPMA communication cap; the group is closed and the
  write-up is not to be forwarded beyond it). The August ask is framed as **charter a
  workgroup** to develop a formal recommendation, with Pathway 1 (SOGI into DCYF
  FamLink first) and Pathway 2 (Office of Equity publishes a binding REALD+SOGI
  standard under RCW 43.06D) as the leading candidates; Pathway 3 (a WA HB
  3159–equivalent statute) needs a legislative champion. *Next action per plan: Evie
  finalizes the write-up and shares it with Sam and the four; content is Evie's.*
- **SB 5599 contingency (G1).** A commission action Evie is bringing forward (not
  personal outreach). Core question: is SB 5599 a keystone protection for LGBTQ minors
  in care, or is there more stable ground if it is struck — mature-minor consent
  rules, the Becca Bill (at-risk-youth / CHINS pathways), DCYF's own processes — and
  where are the gaps? Open DCYF questions: how the shelter-to-DCYF referral works on
  the ground, how often it has been used since enactment, how often a referral ends in
  reunification. The commission (not Evie personally) would engage **Sen. Claire
  Wilson**. *Next action per plan: routed through Sam's agenda flow.*
- **PMP disclosure gap — new topic for commission consideration.** Health-care-data
  privacy / shield-law integrity, distinct from the SOGI collection work. WA's PMP
  (RCW 70.225.020) tracks testosterone; PMP data is confidential but reachable by
  out-of-state subpoena or court order (RCW 70.225.040). Candidate asks: DOH briefing
  on out-of-state PMP subpoenas; notify the Governor's office and LGBTQ Caucus
  contacts; tighten interstate data-sharing to require a warrant (RCW 70.225.040(6));
  Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission standing policy against adding estrogen,
  progesterone, spironolactone, mifepristone or misoprostol absent a specific finding.
  *Next action per plan: introduce via Sam's agenda flow.*
- **SCOTUS watch** — Int'l Partners for Ethical Care v. Ferguson (SB 5599 cert
  granted on standing). *Next action per plan: none until briefing schedule
  publishes; routine monitors.*
- **Ballot initiatives (G3)** — IL26-638 and companion, November 2026. Monitoring
  until fall. Evie's structural read on the June 30 SCOTUS athletes ruling (Little v.
  Hecox / West Virginia v. B.P.J.) is in hand as framing — now a purely state-law
  question with no federal floor either way, so guidance to the Governor is framed as
  "Washington should choose to legislate this way," a symmetric values/policy argument
  rather than a constitutional one. Conversational framing; no action planned.

## Cross-cutting threads

- **Agenda pipeline (governance).** The Commission gets ~5 meetings a year and depends
  on Sam to make intra-government connections — the already-visible bottleneck. Evie's
  posture: follow the flow deliberately to map where it breaks, then build an
  alternative *with* Sam rather than against him. Direction of the fix: empower the
  Chairs to own the agenda (their role) and relocate Sam into a supporting role. For
  each active ask, track two axes — the substance, and what its passage or stall
  reveals about the pipeline — and log what staff commit to versus what happens. Long
  horizon: Evie is working toward the Chair role in year 4 and a faster-acting
  commission culture.
- **Youth safeguarding (precautionary).** Evie has raised, in writing to the Director,
  a precautionary request that the Commission establish abuse-prevention policies and
  training for its youth-facing activity (the intergenerational committee brings
  commissioners into contact with minors). The Director committed to deliver by the
  **August meeting**; Evie is tracking follow-through. The commitment is to the
  outcome — youth are protected — whether the lever is staff action or training. No
  allegations or incidents in the commission context; recorded as a precautionary
  policy matter only.
